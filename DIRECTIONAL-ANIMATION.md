# Directional character animation

Build: `Builds/Directional/Ashfall.exe`.

Verification: dedicated rig checks cover 36 directional weapon poses and all 16 walk frames, including handle attachment and north-facing depth order. The gameplay regression test passed crafting, save/load, boss behavior and all weapon skills. Logs are in `../directional-rig-qa.log` and `../directional-gameplay-qa.log`.

The character now uses distinct front, back, left and right body sprites, with four locomotion frames per direction. The torso faces the aim direction and locks to the attack direction during a swing. Walking advances the leg cycle by distance traveled, not a fixed idle timer. Sword strikes add a short forward step, torso lean and weight shift. Arms attach to the moving shoulders. North-facing weapons render behind the body; side views hide the far arm. Existing controls are unchanged.

Artwork: built-in image-generation tool, reference `Assets/Resources/AshfallHeroRig.png`, saved project asset `Assets/Resources/AshfallHeroDirections.png`. Earlier assets are retained. This is a four-direction system, not eight separately drawn directions; diagonal aiming chooses the closest cardinal body view while the weapon aims continuously.
